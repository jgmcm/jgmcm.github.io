Computational materials theory

James G. McHugh

Dame Kathleen Ollerenshaw Fellow

Department of Physics and Astronomy · University of Manchester

National Graphene Institute · Manchester, UK

I’m a computational materials theorist at the University of Manchester, based in the National Graphene Institute. I develop theoretical, computational and ML methods for materials physics.

I am interested in how atomic-scale structural properties, such as stacking, strain, curvature and interfaces, shape the electronic properties of two-dimensional materials and van der Waals heterostructures.

Please get in touch if you’re interested in applying for competitive PhD or postdoctoral funding to work with me in Manchester, or in collaborating on research.
